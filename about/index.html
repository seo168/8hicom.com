Editorial verification standard

Every material statement is separated into confirmed, pending and instructional information. How the domain network is organised

The main domain owns broad app, account, payment and APK topics. Support domains answer narrower questions and point visitors back to the authoritative route. This structure helps people recognise which page should confirm a file, explain a process or publish a future release notice.

06

Mobile-first publishing principles

Content is written for small screens with short sections, descriptive headings, visible next steps and direct internal links. The goal is not simply to repeat the brand name, but to let a visitor confirm the domain, understand the current status and choose an appropriate guide without unnecessary searching.

07

Information that remains pending

The production APK version, final file size, package identifier, Android requirement and release checksum are not invented before launch. When verified, those fields should be published together so visitors can compare the file on their device with the official record.

08

Update and correction policy

Material corrections should identify what changed and keep technical details consistent across the APK, APK and Support guides. Cosmetic edits do not justify an artificial update date. This approach gives users and search engines a stable record instead of pages whose timestamps change without useful new information.
