Keep entertainment controlled

8HI Game — Responsible Use Guide 2026

Set personal limits before opening a game and stop when play is no longer comfortable. Bonuses, patterns and previous outcomes never guarantee a future result.

Published by 8HICOM · Updated 7 September 2026
01

Set limits first

Choose time and spending boundaries before a session.

02

Recognise warning signs

Pause if play affects sleep, work, relationships or essential expenses.

03

Take action

Use a break, speak with someone you trust and seek qualified help when stopping becomes difficult.

04

Time planning before a session

Choose a start time, stop time and break schedule before opening the app. Short rounds can make elapsed time difficult to notice. Use device reminders and leave when the planned period ends instead of extending the session because of a recent outcome.

05

Money boundaries

Separate entertainment spending from rent, food, education, healthcare, debt payments and savings. Do not borrow, sell necessary items or increase a limit to recover an earlier result. A rebate, commission or Wheelspin reward does not reduce the uncertainty of future play.

06

Behavioural warning signs

Warning signs include hiding activity, losing sleep, missing responsibilities, feeling distressed when unable to play and repeatedly trying to recover losses. Recognising several signs is a reason to stop and seek support, not evidence that a new strategy or promotion will solve the problem.

07

Account-level actions

Use available breaks, limits and self-exclusion controls when needed. Remove saved payment methods, turn off promotional notifications and ask a trusted person for support. Stronger restrictions are appropriate when voluntary reminders are repeatedly ignored.

08

Helping another person

Speak calmly, focus on observable effects and avoid taking control of someone else’s account without consent. Encourage professional help and practical financial safeguards. In an immediate safety crisis, contact appropriate local emergency or health services.
