8HI rewards explained

Exact bonus amounts, qualification rules and limits

The W5W-equivalent 8HI program keeps the same stated values while separating each reward from its eligibility conditions. An eligible agent reward is ₹100, the mentor component is ₹50 through the official Telegram process, and the qualifying downline amount is ₹20. The downline must deposit at least ₹200 and complete ₹600 turnover. A displayed offer is not proof that an account has qualified; users should confirm progress and settlement in the verified account record.

01

First-deposit rebate levels

The published levels are 2% without a star, 3% at one star, 4% at two stars and 5% at three stars. Eligibility, calculation base and credited status should be checked before relying on an estimated amount.

02

Turnover commission chain

The four stated levels are 0.2%, 0.3%, 0.45% and 0.6%. Each percentage belongs to a specific invitation depth; it should not be treated as one combined rate or as a guaranteed return.
