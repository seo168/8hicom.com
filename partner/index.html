How partner listings are evaluated

A partner entry should identify its official name, intended service, destination and relationship to 8HI. The directory must distinguish confirmed relationships from general references. A logo, shared colour palette or copied reward table is not enough evidence of an authorised connection.

05

Platform-code context

The supplied reward reference includes W5W at ₹100 and other agent codes with different amounts. For 8HI content, only the W5W-equivalent ₹100 amount and universal program rules are carried forward. Unrelated platform-code amounts are not presented as 8HI rewards.

06

Universal program components

Universal benefits include the ₹20 downline reward, first-deposit rebate levels of 2%, 3%, 4% and 5%, and turnover commission levels of 0.2%, 0.3%, 0.45% and 0.6%. The qualification base and account relationship should be verified before calculation.

07

Invitation Wheelspin relationship

An agent may receive a wheelspin after the invited downline deposits during the three-day activity. The displayed ₹500 is the maximum possible result, not a fixed payment. Eligibility, timing and the actual wheel result should be visible in the applicable platform record.

08

Safe movement between platforms

Use a partner directory only as a navigation aid. Before entering account information, depositing or downloading, verify the destination independently. Do not reuse passwords between platforms, and do not assume that support for one brand can recover an account belonging to another.
